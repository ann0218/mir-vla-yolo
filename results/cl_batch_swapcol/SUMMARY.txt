Reading
- After swapping the colours the policy follows the named COLOUR, not the prim it
  followed before: 11/11 pairs on both scores. Since the two people are the same
  character, the swap rules out cues tied to the prim -- walker index, gait,
  trajectory, which TF side it starts on.
- Performance did not drop with the swap (p = 0.94 on the same seeds).
- Not shown: that the model grounds colour words it was never trained on. Only
  purple and yellow were ever seen; an unseen colour (red) is the test for that.
