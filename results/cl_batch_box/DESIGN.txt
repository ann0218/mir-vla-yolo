and does its predicted box tell us why it fails when it fails?

Read as follows

  box good, control worse       the 7-dim loss spends capacity on boxes; report the trade
  box poor in closed loop but   the model localises when shown expert-like frames and

    person while it turns       came from picking the wrong person, not from bad steering
