Reading
- The sensor-only safety layer did what it was for: no tip-overs, and close approaches to
  the distractor fell from 184 to 42 steps. It never uses ground truth.
- It is not complete: the robot still came within 0.59 m, mostly of people outside the
  camera's central band -- beside it, where neither the camera stop nor the lidar (which
  cannot see people) reaches. Close to the target itself it is about unchanged (46 -> 53).
- The policy still follows the named colour (11/12 pairs, p = 0.001 on both scores), with
  all following numbers slightly worse than without the layer; none of those differences
  is significant on 11 pairs. The layer intervenes a lot (acceleration limit on ~24 % of
  steps), which is the likely cost.
- Next, if needed: widen the camera stop to the whole frame for slow speeds, or add a rule
  for people near the frame edge; and consider the acceleration limit's effect on following.
