Driving batch with sensor-based safety -- design, written 2026-09-15 BEFORE the batch
(smoke_safe.csv, one 40 s run, was used only to check plumbing and calibrate the camera
stop; it is not part of any result)

Question: does the policy still follow the named person once the safety layer no longer
relies on ground truth, and does that layer keep it clear of people and upright?

Baseline to compare with: cl_batch_drive (same checkpoint, scene, seeds, avoidance, arm,
--max-w 0.6, --max-dw 1.2). Changes in this batch, and only these:

  1. forward-acceleration limit  --max-accel 1.0 (m/s^2, i.e. +0.1 m/s per tick).
     Speed-ups while moving forward only; braking and reversing untouched.
     Why: s11_yellow tipped after v jumped +0.52 m/s in one tick. The expert makes such
     jumps too (10.7 % of its forward speed-ups exceed 0.3 m/s per tick), so this limit
     will slow ~21-23 % of forward speed-ups -- a deliberate departure from the data.
  2. wall limit on the TRUE forward arc  --true-front
     The expert's front_clear() and the previous batch watched the rear arc (beam 0 of
     /scan points backwards). Same thresholds: slow from 1.6 m, stop at 0.7 m, 90 deg arc.
  3. camera stop instead of ground-truth stop  --cam-stop-y2 470 --cam-stop-halfdeg 25
     No forward speed while a YOLO person box whose centre is within +-25 deg of the axis
     reaches image row 470 (frame bottom 480). The lidar cannot see the people at all.
     Calibrated on smoke_safe.csv, steps with exactly one person inside +-25 deg:
        true distance 1.6-2.0 m  n=5   box bottom median 479.6   >= 470 in 100 %
        true distance 2.0-2.5 m  n=8   box bottom median 462.5   >= 470 in   0 %
        true distance 2.5-3.5 m  n=17  box bottom median 382.3   >= 470 in   0 %
     So it fires at roughly <= 2.0 m, not the ~1.55 m estimated from camera geometry;
     no samples under 1.6 m. More conservative than the expert's 1.7 m back-off.
  4. ground truth logged, NOT acted on  --estop-dist 0 --gt-monitor-dist 1.0
     gt_close counts steps with a person < 1.0 m ahead -- the steps the previous batch's
     emergency stop would have caught.

Everything else as cl_batch_drive: runner_2p.py --drive --seconds 70 --warmup 25 --trace,
12 seeds, odd seeds swap sides, words purple / yellow, target = person wearing the word.

Scores
  instruction  on_target sum and in-view named-minus-other, paired over seeds
  following    named-person distance mean, % within 1.5-3.0 m, % named person in view
  safety       gt_close steps and runs, closest approach, camera stops, acceleration
               clips, tip-overs
  comparison   same seeds vs cl_batch_drive for all of the above

No pass/fail threshold; p-values reported. Because four things change at once, a
difference from cl_batch_drive is attributed to the safety layer as a whole, not to any
single part of it.
